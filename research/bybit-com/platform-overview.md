## 2. Full Product Suite on Bybit.com

Bybit.com offers a comprehensive, institutional-grade crypto and derivative product suite:

### A. Spot Trading & Market Innovations
* **Standard Spot Trading:** 400+ cryptocurrency pairs against USDT, USDC, BTC, and fiat currencies.
* **Convert:** Zero-fee instant crypto-to-crypto conversion.
* **Pre-Market Trading:** Over-the-counter (OTC) platform allowing traders to trade new tokens before official spot listing.
* **TradFi Stock Perpetuals & Pre-IPO:** Perpetual contracts based on major US stocks (e.g., Apple, Tesla, Nvidia) and Pre-IPO assets (e.g., Shein).

### B. Derivatives Trading
* **USDT Perpetuals:** Linear perpetual contracts settled and margined in USDT.
* **USDC Perpetuals & Futures:** Linear perpetuals settled in USDC.
* **Inverse Perpetuals & Futures:** Coin-margined contracts (e.g., BTCUSD, ETHUSD) where cryptocurrency serves as collateral and margin settlement.
* **USDC Options:** European-style cash-settled options for BTC, ETH, and SOL.

### C. Margin Trading & Account Infrastructure
* **Spot Margin Trading:** Up to 10x leverage on spot assets.
* **Unified Trading Account (UTA):** Multi-currency collateral account consolidating Spot, Spot Margin, USDT Perpetuals, USDC Perpetuals, and Options under a single portfolio margin balance, offering up to 100x leverage on futures.

### D. Bybit Earn & Wealth Management
* **Bybit Savings:** Flexible and fixed-term yield products for major cryptocurrencies.
* **Dual Asset Investment:** Structured non-principal-protected product yielding enhanced returns based on price direction targets.
* **Liquidity Mining:** Yield generation through automated market maker (AMM) liquidity pools.
* **Shark Fin & Discount Buy:** Structured products offering capital preservation or discounted asset purchases.
* **On-Chain Earn & RWA Earn:** Direct protocol staking and tokenized Real-World Asset (RWA) yield options (e.g., US Treasury yields, nOPAL).

### E. Crypto Loans & Institutional Borrowing
* **Crypto Loans:** Retail borrowing against crypto collateral with flexible repayment schedules.
* **Premier Loans:** Institutional collateralized borrowing with custom loan-to-value (LTV) ratios.

### F. Copy Trading
* **Copy Trading Platform:** Allows Followers to automatically replicate trades executed by vetted Master Traders across Futures and Spot markets, with profit-sharing mechanisms (10%–15% for Master Traders).

### G. Trading Bots
* **Spot Grid Bot:** Automated grid buying/selling within preset price corridors.
* **Futures Grid Bot:** Leveraged grid trading for long/short positioning.
* **DCA Bot (Dollar-Cost Averaging):** Automated recurring investment bot.
* **Futures Martingale Bot:** Position doubling strategy engine for mean-reversion trading.

### H. Launchpad, Launchpool & ByStarter
* **Launchpad:** Subscription-based early token distribution using Mantle (MNT) or USDT lotteries.
* **Launchpool:** Staking pools where users stake MNT, USDT, or new tokens to earn free new project allocations.

### I. Bybit Web3 & Wallet
* **Bybit Wallet:** Cloud Wallet, Keyless MPC (Multi-Party Computation) Wallet, and Seed Phrase Wallet.
* **Web3 Ecosystem:** Multi-chain DEX aggregator, NFT Marketplace, and Web3 Earn staking portal.

### J. Bybit Card (Global Version)
* **Debit Card:** Issued via Mastercard or Visa network, linking directly to the user's Bybit Funding Account.
* **Features:** Instant crypto-to-fiat conversion at checkout, cashback rewards up to 10% in VIP tiers, points program, and multi-fiat spending.

## 3. Bybit EU vs Bybit.com Differences

This section highlights the critical regulatory, structural, and product boundaries separating **Bybit.com** (Global) from **Bybit EU** (`bybit.eu`). **This is the primary reference section for Bybit EU customer support agents.**

| Feature / Dimension | Bybit.com (Global Platform) | Bybit EU (`bybit.eu`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operating Entity** | Bybit Fintech Limited / Offshore operating entities | **Bybit EU GmbH** (Vienna, Austria) |
| **Regulatory Status** | Dubai VARA (regional MEA) / Offshore (BVI) | **MiCAR-Compliant CASP** (Austrian Financial Market Authority - FMA), passported across 29 EEA countries |
| **Derivatives & Leverage** | Full access (USDT/USDC Perpetuals, Options, Inverse Futures, up to 100x UTA leverage) | **PROHIBITED** under MiCA CASP regulations (No Futures, No Perpetuals, No Options, No 100x leverage). *(Note: Bybit EU Group has established Bybit X GmbH to apply for MiFID II for future regulated derivatives)* |
| **Spot Markets** | 400+ crypto trading pairs, Stock Perpetuals, Pre-Market derivatives | Regulated Spot pairs focusing on EUR fiat pairs and major assets (BTC, ETH, SOL, MiCA-compliant stablecoins) |
| **Stablecoin Rules** | Unrestricted support for USDT, USDC, USDE, etc. | Strict MiCAR Title III compliance: non-compliant stablecoins (e.g., USDT) subject to EEA restrictions/phase-out in favor of compliant stablecoins (USDC, EUR-backed tokens) |
| **Earn & Wealth Suite** | Full suite (Dual Asset, Shark Fin, Discount Buy, Unbacked Loans, High-yield Earn) | **Restricted / MiCA-compliant Earn only** (Staking & fixed savings with strict capital backing rules; no speculative unbacked structured products) |
| **Fiat Gateways** | Global P2P, Credit Card, Third-Party Providers | **Integrated EU Banking Gateways**: Direct SEPA & SEPA Instant EUR deposits/withdrawals, EUR Debit/Credit Card |
| **Bybit Card** | Global Mastercard/Visa (multi-currency fiat conversion) | **Bybit EU Card** (EUR-denominated debit card compliant with EEA payment services directives) |
| **Asset Safeguards** | Offshore reserve management (Proof of Reserves auditing) | **1:1 Asset Segregation** in Tier-1 EEA custodian accounts with statutory capital reserves under MiCAR |
| **KYC & Onboarding** | Tiered global KYC requirements | **Mandatory Strict MiCA Level 1/2 KYC** prior to any deposit/trade; full AML5/6 & Travel Rule compliance |
| **Dispute Resolution** | Mandatory international arbitration (SIAC / HKIAC / BVI Courts) | **EU Consumer Protection Laws**; jurisdiction under Austrian courts and national competent financial ombudsmen |

### Key Support Talking Points for EU Customers:
1. **Why can't EU users trade Futures or Perpetuals on bybit.com?**
   * Under the European Union’s Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCAR), Crypto-Asset Service Providers (CASPs) like Bybit EU GmbH cannot offer unregulated crypto derivatives. European residents are redirected to `bybit.eu` to ensure full compliance with EU investor protection laws.
2. **Can an EU customer keep an account on Bybit.com?**
   * No. EEA residents must complete verification under `bybit.eu`. Global platform services are restricted for residents of the EEA as part of regulatory transition agreements.
3. **What happens to USDT on Bybit EU?**
   * Under MiCAR, electronic money tokens (EMTs) must be issued by licensed e-money institutions. Bybit EU prioritizes MiCAR-compliant stablecoins (such as USDC and EUR stablecoins) and compliant EUR spot pairs.

## 5. Bybit.com Terms & Conditions Highlights

Bybit.com’s Terms of Service govern global non-EU users. Understanding these terms helps explain why certain global policies do not apply to Bybit EU clients:

### A. Excluded & Restricted Jurisdictions
* Bybit.com strictly excludes residents and citizens of: **United States, Mainland China, Singapore, Canada, Hong Kong, North Korea, Cuba, Iran, Syria, Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk**, and the **United Kingdom** (for retail derivatives).
* Residents of **EEA countries** are prohibited from using Bybit.com and are routed exclusively to `bybit.eu`.

### B. Governing Law & Dispute Settlement
* **Governing Law:** Governed primarily by the laws of the **British Virgin Islands (BVI)** or local offshore corporate entities (e.g., UAE VARA framework for Dubai entity).
* **Arbitration:** All disputes must be settled through **mandatory, binding individual arbitration** (e.g., Singapore International Arbitration Centre - SIAC, or HKIAC) with explicit class-action waivers. Customers waive rights to local court litigation.

### C. Automated Liquidation & Auto-Deleveraging (ADL)
* Bybit.com terms explicitly outline automatic margin call liquidation engines and **Auto-Deleveraging (ADL)** mechanisms. If the Insurance Fund cannot cover bankrupt position losses during extreme market volatility, opposing profitable positions are forcibly deleveraged based on profit and leverage ranking without platform compensation.

### D. Limitation of Liability
* Terms contain broad disclaimers absolving Bybit of liability for trading losses, market slippage, API outages, scheduled maintenance, or cyber security incidents.

### E. Contrast with Bybit EU T&Cs
* **Bybit EU T&Cs** operate under Austrian law and EU directives. They grant statutory consumer rights, mandatory transparent order execution rules, strict 1:1 asset backing, and access to European regulatory complaint channels (FMA Austria / EU ombudsmen).
