# Bybit EU: Legal, Terms & Compliance Knowledge Base

This document serves as a reference manual for customer support AI copilots and agents assisting users on **Bybit EU** (`bybit.eu`). It details the legal, regulatory, compliance, and procedural frameworks operating under European Union regulations, including the **Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCAR)**.

## 1. Bybit EU Terms & Conditions / User Agreement

The Bybit EU Platform Terms & Conditions and General Terms govern all interactions between European Economic Area (EEA) users and the platform.

### Key Clauses for Support Agents

#### A. Account Registration & Eligibility Rules
* **Age Requirement**: Users must be at least 18 years of age and possess full legal capacity.
* **Residency Requirement**: Available exclusively to residents of eligible EEA jurisdictions. Residents of specified restricted countries (e.g., Malta, USA) are barred from accessing services.
* **Verification (KYC)**: Completion of identity verification (Level 1 KYC minimum) is mandatory before opening an account, depositing funds, or placing trades.
* **Single Account Rule**: Users may only maintain one main account. Creating multiple accounts without prior authorization is prohibited and subject to immediate suspension.

#### B. Prohibited Conduct
Bybit EU strictly prohibits activities that compromise platform integrity or breach law:
* **Market Abuse & Manipulation**: Wash trading, spoofing, front-running, price manipulation, or insider trading.
* **Sanctions Evasion & Financial Crime**: Utilizing the platform for money laundering, terrorism financing, fraud, or evading international sanctions.
* **Technical Abuse**: Automated scraping, unauthorized API access, exploitation of software bugs, or distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks.
* **Commercial Resale**: Commercial exploitation or resale of Bybit EU services without written authorization.

#### C. Limitation of Liability
* **"As Is" Basis**: Services are provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis without warranties of uninterrupted uptime or error-free operation.
* **Exclusion of Indirect Damages**: Bybit EU excludes liability for lost profits, loss of opportunity, trading losses, indirect, or consequential damages.
* **Operational Disruption**: Bybit EU is not liable for losses resulting from blockchain network congestion, hard forks, third-party bank outages, or force majeure events.
* **Statutory Liability Caps**: Where liability cannot be fully excluded under mandatory EU consumer protection laws, liability is limited to fees collected from the user in the preceding 12-month period.

#### D. Termination & Account Suspension
* **Suspension Rights**: Bybit EU reserves the right to immediately suspend, restrict, or freeze an account without prior notice under circumstances including:
  * Reasonable suspicion of illegal activity, money laundering, or fraud.
  * Failure to pass periodic KYC/AML re-verification or Source of Funds (SoF) checks.
  * Breach of General Terms or Platform T&Cs.
  * Orders or demands issued by regulatory authorities or law enforcement.
* **User-Initiated Termination**: Users may close their account at any time, provided all outstanding debts, margin positions, and negative balances are fully settled prior to closure.

## 2. Privacy Policy & Data Protection (GDPR)

Bybit EU processes personal data in accordance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 - GDPR)** and Austrian data protection law (*Datenschutzgesetz - DSG*).

### Data Controller & Contact
* **Data Controller**: **Bybit EU GmbH**, Donau-City-Straße 7, 1220 Vienna, Austria.
* **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: Contactable via email at `dpo@bybit.eu` or through formal support ticket submission.

### Categories of Personal Data Collected
1. **Identity & Verification Data**: Full name, date of birth, nationality, government ID details, proof of address, selfie/biometric liveness checks.
2. **Financial & Transaction Data**: Bank account numbers, transaction histories, crypto wallet addresses, source of funds (SoF) and source of wealth (SoW) evidence.
3. **Technical & Telemetry Data**: IP addresses, device identifiers, login timestamps, operating system, browser specifications, and cookie data.
4. **Communications Data**: Customer support tickets, chat logs, email correspondence, and formal complaint records.

### Data Retention Periods
* **General Rule**: Personal data is retained for the duration of the active business relationship plus applicable statutory retention periods.
* **AML/MiCA Mandatory Retention**: Under Article 4 of the RTS on Record-Keeping (Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1140) and Austrian ZaDiG 2018, transaction records, identity verification data, and complaint records must be retained for **a minimum of 5 years** following the termination of the customer relationship or account closure.

### User Rights Under GDPR
EEA residents possess statutory rights regarding their personal data:
* **Access (Art. 15)**: Request confirmation of data processing and copies of personal data.
* **Rectification (Art. 16)**: Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
* **Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Art. 17)**: Request deletion of personal data when processing is no longer necessary.
* **Restriction of Processing (Art. 18)**: Request temporary restriction of data processing during disputes.
* **Data Portability (Art. 20)**: Receive personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
* **Objection (Art. 21)**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
* **Regulatory Complaint**: Lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (*Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde - DSB*, `www.dsb.gv.at`).

### Process for Data Deletion Requests
* **Submission**: Users must submit data deletion requests via email to `dpo@bybit.eu` or through the online case webform.
* **Legal Retention Limitation**: **Critical Note for Support Agents**: Support agents must inform users that requesting account deletion or data erasure does *not* result in immediate total deletion of transaction and KYC records. Legal requirements under EU AML/CTF legislation and MiCA mandate that identity and transaction logs be stored securely for **at least 5 years** post-account closure.

## 3. Risk Disclosures & Regulatory Warnings

Under MiCAR framework requirements, crypto-asset service providers must furnish clear, prominent, and non-misleading risk disclosures.

### Mandatory EU Regulatory Risk Disclosure
Bybit EU displays required risk warnings across web and mobile platforms:

> **Warning**: Crypto-assets are highly volatile and unregulated digital assets. Your capital is at risk. You may lose the entire value of your investment. Crypto-assets are not covered by European Deposit Guarantee Schemes (DGS) or Investor Compensation Schemes (ICS).

### Key Risk Categories Documented

#### 1. Market & Volatility Risk
Crypto-asset prices fluctuate wildly over short timeframes. Past performance is not indicative of future returns. High market volatility can lead to substantial financial losses.

#### 2. Margin & Leverage Risk (Spot Margin Trading)
Spot margin trading (up to 10x leverage) carries amplified risk. Rapid market movements toward liquidation prices can trigger automated position liquidations. Users are fully responsible for maintaining required margin ratios.

#### 3. No Deposit Protection
Crypto assets and fiat funds held in crypto-asset wallets do not benefit from statutory deposit insurance schemes (such as the Austrian *Einlagensicherung*).

#### 4. Liquidity & Technological Risk
Low liquidity in specific pairs may prevent trade execution at expected prices. Software glitches, smart contract vulnerabilities, blockchain forks, or network disruption can impede order placement or withdrawals.

## 5. Complaints & Dispute Resolution

Bybit EU has established a structured Complaints Handling Policy compliant with European regulatory guidelines.

### How a Customer Files a Complaint

#### 1. Channels for Submission
* **Online Case Submission Webform**: Primary recommended channel at `https://www.bybit.eu/en-EU/help-center/s/webform`
* **Live Chat**: 24/7 available chat on the website or mobile app.
* **Postal Mail**: Written complaints sent to:
  * *Bybit EU GmbH* (or *Bybit Payments GmbH*), Attention: Customer Support - Complaints, Donau-City-Straße 7, 1220 Vienna, Austria.

#### 2. Mandatory Admissibility Requirements
To be deemed admissible, a complaint **must** include:
1. User ID (UID)
2. Registered Email Address
3. Relevant Transaction ID and/or Order ID (if applicable)
4. Detailed description of the issue
5. Supporting documentation/evidence (screenshots, bank statements)

*Note*: Failure to provide required fields renders the complaint **inadmissible**. Filing and handling are entirely free of charge.

### Handling Timelines & Stages
1. **Acknowledgement (Within 5 Working Days)**: Written receipt confirmation issued with a unique case tracking ID.
2. **Admissibility Review**: Immediate check of complainant identity and mandatory submission fields.
3. **Internal Investigation**: Escalation to relevant technical, financial, or compliance teams.
4. **Final Decision (Within 15 Working Days)**: Formal written decision issued detailing findings and resolution steps.
5. **Exceptional Extension (Up to 35 Working Days Maximum)**: In complex cases requiring extended research, an interim status update explaining reasons for delay must be issued within 15 working days, with a final resolution delivered within 35 working days.

## 6. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF) Highlights

Bybit EU enforces robust AML/CTF protocols in compliance with EU Anti-Money Laundering Directives (AMLD) and Austrian legislation (*FM-GwG*).

### Core AML Requirements
* **Mandatory Identity Verification**: No trading, deposits, or withdrawals are permitted without verified identity checks.
* **Source of Funds (SoF) & Source of Wealth (SoW)**: Users exceeding transaction thresholds or flagged by risk rules must provide verified proof of funds (e.g., bank statements, tax returns, payslips, or sale deeds).
* **Automated Sanctions Screening**: Continuous real-time screening against international sanctions lists, including EU, UN, OFAC, and UK sanctions regimes. Accounts matching sanctioned lists are frozen immediately.
* **Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)**: Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) applied to PEPs, their family members, and close associates.
* **Market Surveillance**: Real-time integration of institutional-grade market surveillance tools (such as Nasdaq Market Surveillance) to monitor trading patterns for manipulative conduct or suspicious transaction flows.

## 7. Refund Policy & Chargeback Handling

### Crypto Asset Transactions
* **Irreversibility**: Due to the nature of distributed ledger technology, all completed crypto transfers, blockchain deposits, and executed market trades are final and non-refundable.

### Fiat Deposits & Bank Transfers
* **Failed / Rejected Deposits**: Fiat deposits sent from bank accounts where the account owner's name does not match the verified Bybit EU account name are rejected and returned to the originating bank account, subject to processing or bank inter-agency fees.
* **Withdrawal Errors**: Incorrect bank details submitted during withdrawal requests will result in returned funds once rejected by the destination bank, minus applicable administrative fees.

### Card Purchases & Bybit Card Chargebacks
* **Transaction Refunds**: Refunds processed by merchants on the Bybit EU Card are credited back to the user's fiat balance. Corresponding reward points earned on the original purchase will be deducted.
* **Chargeback Abuse / Friendly Fraud**: Submitting unauthorized or fraudulent chargebacks with card issuers against legitimate platform transactions is a violation of T&Cs. Bybit EU reserves the right to suspend accounts, offset negative balances against crypto holdings, or initiate legal recovery.

## 8. Common Customer Issues & Agent Action Protocols

This section outlines protocols for support agents and AI copilots when responding to common customer inquiries or escalations.

### Scenario A: Account Frozen / Restricted / Pending Compliance Review

#### Root Causes
1. Routine AML risk triggers or identity document expiration requiring updated KYC.
2. Unfulfilled request for Source of Funds (SoF) / Source of Wealth (SoW) documentation.
3. Suspicious IP login location or security threat flag.
4. Name match or false-positive hit on sanctions or PEP screening databases.

#### Support Agent Protocol
1. **Communication Safety**: Do **not** disclose specific compliance risk scores or internal flagging details to prevent "tipping off" under AML laws.
2. **User Guidance**: Inform the user that their account is undergoing a standard compliance or security review.
3. **Action Instructions**: Direct the user to check their email or verification hub in the Bybit EU portal to submit the required documentation (e.g., recent bank statement, proof of income, updated ID).
4. **SLA Expectations**: Advise the user that compliance reviews typically take between 5 to 15 business days following receipt of complete documentation.

---

### Scenario B: Customer Filing a Formal Complaint

#### Root Causes
1. Unresolved technical or deposit issue exceeding standard customer support timelines.
2. Dispute over trade execution, system latency during high volatility, or liquidation.
3. Disagreements regarding card transactions or fee deductions.

#### Support Agent Protocol
1. **Provide Official Portal Link**: Share the direct webform link: `https://www.bybit.eu/en-EU/help-center/s/webform`.
2. **Review Mandatory Admissibility Criteria**: Remind the customer that to process their complaint without rejection, they must include:
   * UID
   * Registered Email
   * Transaction ID / Order ID
   * Full problem statement & supporting evidence (e.g., screenshots)
3. **Explain Response Timeline**:
   * Initial acknowledgement within **5 working days**.
   * Final decision within **15 working days** (or max **35 working days** for complex cases).
4. **Escalation Path**: Inform the customer that if they remain dissatisfied after receiving the final decision, they have the right to escalate free of charge to the Austrian *Bankenschlichtung*, *Verbraucherschlichtung*, or the EU ODR platform.

---

### Scenario C: GDPR Data Requests & Account Deletion Requests

#### Root Causes
1. User requesting full deletion of personal data ("Right to be Forgotten").
2. User requesting a Subject Access Request (SAR) for a copy of their personal data.

#### Support Agent Protocol
1. **Submission Path**: Direct the user to email `dpo@bybit.eu` or submit a request via the help center webform selecting data privacy.
2. **Clarify Retention Rules**: Explain clearly and politely that while account access will be terminated upon closure, Bybit EU is legally mandated under EU MiCA regulation and AML/CTF laws (Article 4 CDR (EU) 2025/1140 & Austrian ZaDiG 2018) to retain identity verification records and transaction histories for **a minimum of 5 years** post-account closure.
