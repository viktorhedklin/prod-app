# Bybit Card (EEA) Knowledge Base & Customer Support Guide

## 1. How to Apply for a Bybit Card (EU Site)

### Eligibility & Requirements
The Bybit Card is an EEA-regulated Mastercard debit card issued by Bybit EU (bybit.eu) that allows users to spend fiat and crypto directly from their Bybit EU Funding Account.

* **Supported Jurisdictions**:
  * **Standard Virtual & Physical Cards**: Available to residents of European Economic Area (EEA) countries: all 27 EU member states plus Norway, Liechtenstein, and Iceland.
  * **Virtual Card Lite**: Available exclusively to residents in the EU region (excluding Croatia and Ireland) and Norway.
* **Identity Verification (Proof of Identity - POI)**:
  * Accepted documents: National ID Card, Passport, EU (excluding Croatia and Ireland) or Norway Residence Permit, or EU (excluding Croatia and Ireland) or Norway Driving License.
  * Documents must be valid and unexpired. In select security scenarios, users may be required to submit two separate forms of POI.
* **Address Verification (Proof of Address - POA)**:
  * Accepted documents: Utility bills, bank statements, tax returns, or official government correspondence issued within the last 3 months.
  * Must clearly display the user's full name, residential address, and issue date within a supported EEA country.
  * In certain low-risk cases, manual address entry without uploading a document is permitted.

### Application Steps

#### A. Apply for a Virtual Bybit Card
1. **Navigate to Card Hub**: Log in to your Bybit EU account, go to **Finance** → **Card**, and click **Get Your Card**.
2. **Select Residence**: Choose your official Country of Residence. (If unsupported, interest will be registered for future expansion).
3. **Complete Eligibility Check**:
   * **Identity Verification**: Click **Verify** and upload your accepted POI document.
   * **Address Verification**: Select the issuing country of your POA and upload document (or enter residential address if prompted).
4. **Security & Profile Setup**: Confirm your registered email address, set up Google 2FA (if not already enabled), and optionally enter a Referral Code. *Note: Once confirmed, the referral code cannot be changed.*
5. **Review & Approval**: Virtual card applications are reviewed automatically within a few minutes. Manual review cases take up to **7 business days**. Approval notifications are sent via email.

#### B. Apply for a Physical Bybit Card
*Prerequisite: User must successfully obtain an approved Virtual Bybit Card first.*
1. **Initiate Physical Application**: Go to **Finance** → **Card** and click on the **Physical Card Application** banner.
2. **Card Display Name**: Edit the embossed display name on the card (up to 21 Latin characters and spaces).
3. **Shipping Address**: Confirm or edit the delivery shipping address. *Crucial rule: The shipping address must strictly match the address approved during Virtual Card Address Verification. Additional POAs to change delivery address are not accepted during application.*
4. **Set Card PIN**: Create and verify a 4-digit numeric PIN for ATM withdrawals and retail chip-and-PIN payments.
5. **Submit & Review**: Click **Apply**. Address review takes up to **7 business days**.
6. **Final Confirmation**: Once address review is approved, return to the application page and click **Apply** to trigger card dispatch.
7. **Delivery & Tracking**:
   * **Standard Delivery**: Free shipping taking up to **30 days**. Standard shipping is untracked.
   * **VIP Shipping (DHL)**: Available to VIP tier users. DHL delivery includes a tracking number on the Card Management page.
   * **Tax ID Requirement for DHL**: Cyprus, Estonia, Finland, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, and Spain require a personal Tax ID for DHL customs clearance.

### Common Customer Issues
* **Application Stuck in Review (> 7 Days)**: Ensure emails from `notification@bybit.eu` and `compliance@bybit.eu` are not blocked or sent to spam. If 7 business days have passed, escalate via Support Hub.
* **Shipping Address Rejection**: Occurs when the entered delivery address differs from the Address Verification POA. The user must re-submit the address matching their original POA.
* **Forgot to Input Referral Code**: Referral codes must be entered during Step 4 before clicking Confirm. They cannot be retroactively applied after card issuance.
* **Physical Card Not Delivered After 30 Days**: Standard delivery offers no tracking. If 30 days have elapsed since approval, instruct the customer to verify their address and request a card replacement on the Card Management page.

## 2. How to Activate Your Bybit Card (EEA)

### Activation Procedures

#### A. Virtual Bybit Card Activation
* **Automatic Activation**: The Virtual Card is automatically activated immediately upon application approval, provided profile settings are complete.
* **Prerequisite Profile Settings**: Users must complete their profile under **Card Dashboard** → **Settings**:
  1. Billing Address (used for card verification, separate from main Bybit account address).
  2. Registered Mobile Number (used for transaction OTPs).
  3. Security Questions (used for payment authentication).

#### B. Physical Bybit Card Activation
Physical cards must be manually activated upon receipt before any offline or online payments can be made.
1. **Access Card Management**: Navigate to **Finance** → **Card** → click **Manage My Card**.
2. **Initiate Activation**: Click **Activate**.
3. **Complete 2FA Verification**:
   * **Email Verification**: Click *Send Verification Code* and input the 6-digit code sent to your registered email.
   * **Google 2FA**: Input the 6-digit code from Google Authenticator and click **Confirm**.
4. **CVV Input**: Enter the 3-digit CVV printed on the back of your physical Bybit Card and click **Activate**.
*(Note: If Billing Address, Mobile Number, or Security Questions were not completed prior, the system will prompt for these inputs before CVV verification).*

### Mandatory Post-Activation PIN Initialization
To enable contactless payments and online merchant compatibility with a new physical card (or after a PIN reset):
1. **ATM Transaction First**: Insert physical card into an ATM and perform a transaction using your 4-digit PIN (a simple **Balance Inquiry** is sufficient).
2. **Chip & PIN Retail Purchase**: Perform a physical purchase at a retail merchant terminal inserting the chip and entering the PIN.
3. **Contactless Unlocked**: Contactless tap-to-pay and secondary transaction channels are fully unlocked only after completing steps 1 and 2.

### Common Customer Issues
* **Unable to Click Activate**: Customer has not received the physical card yet and lacks the 3-digit CVV required for activation.
* **Contactless Payments Declined Immediately After Activation**: The customer skipped the mandatory ATM PIN initialization / chip-and-PIN transaction step. Instruct the customer to perform an ATM balance inquiry first.
* **OTP / 2FA Code Not Received**: Check junk folders, whitelist `notification@bybit.eu`, or ensure Google Authenticator time synchronization is correct.
* **Profile Settings Discrepancy**: Mobile number or billing address updated on main Bybit EU account does NOT automatically update Card Settings; user must explicitly set them in Card Dashboard → Settings.

## 3. Bybit Card General Inquiries (FAQ)

### Key Specifications & Architecture
* **Card Issuer & Type**: EEA-regulated Mastercard debit card denominated in Euros (**EUR**).
* **Funding Mechanism**: Payments are debited directly from the user's **Bybit EU Funding Account**. Supported funding assets include Fiat (EUR) and supported crypto assets (USDC, USDT, BTC, ETH, XRP, etc.).
* **Multiple Card Coexistence**: A user can hold **one (1) Virtual Card** and **one (1) Physical Card** simultaneously.
* **Virtual Card Lite Specifications**: Lightweight virtual card available in eligible EU regions with a non-upgradable **150 EUR lifetime spending cap**.
* **Global Acceptance**: Works worldwide at over 90 million merchants wherever Mastercard is accepted. No international travel notices are required prior to spending abroad.

### Delivery & Shipping FAQ
* **Standard Shipping**: Free of charge, delivered via local postal networks within **30 days**. No tracking provided.
* **VIP DHL Shipping**: Includes tracking. Tracking numbers appear on the Card Management page under physical card status once shipped.
* **Wrong Address Post-Order**: If an incorrect shipping address was entered and the card has not arrived, click **Incorrect Address?** on the Card Management page to terminate the order, update address details, and re-apply.

### Common Customer Issues
* **Declined Due to Balance in Trading Account**: Bybit Card reads funds **EXCLUSIVELY** from the **Funding Account**. Funds in Unified Trading Account (UTA), Derivatives, or Earn Accounts will not be spent. Users must transfer funds to Funding Account.
* **Attempting to Order Multiple Physical Cards**: Only 1 active physical card per account is permitted. Users must terminate or report lost an existing physical card before ordering a replacement.
* **Inquiries Before 30 Delivery Days Elapsed**: Support cannot issue replacement cards or trace untracked standard shipments prior to the full 30-day delivery window expiring.

## 4. Bybit Card Rewards

### Rewards Program Mechanics
Bybit EU operates a points-based loyalty program where card spending earns **Rewards Points**.

* **Base Point Value**: **1 Rewards Point = 0.002 EUR value**.
* **Points Accrual Timeline**: Points earned from transactions remain in **Pending** status for **4 calendar days** following transaction settlement. After 4 days, they convert to **Available Points** ready for redemption or cashback.
* **Points Expiration**: Rewards Points **DO NOT EXPIRE**.
* **Base & VIP Multipliers**:
  * Non-VIP users earn **1% flat cashback equivalent** (5 points per EUR spent).
  * VIP Tiers (VIP 1 to Supreme VIP) receive higher point multipliers and higher monthly point caps.
* **100% Merchant Rebates**: VIP cardholders enjoy 100% rebates on selected partner subscriptions (e.g., Netflix, Spotify, TradingView, Prime) when subscribing directly on partner websites using the Bybit Card.

### Non-Eligible Transactions (Zero Points Earned)
Rewards Points are **NOT** awarded for the following transaction types:
1. **Fees & Charges**: Annual card fees, administrative charges, service fees.
2. **ATM Cash Withdrawals**: Automated cash disbursements (MCC 6011).
3. **Financial / Merchandise Disbursements**: Debt repayments, merchandise, and services at financial institutions (MCC 6012).
4. **Prepaid Card & Stored Value Top-Ups**: Stored value card loads, prepaid top-ups (MCC 6540).
5. **Foreign Currency & Money Orders**: Foreign currency, non-fiat, money orders, account funding, travelers cheques (MCC 6051).
6. **Money Transfers**: Peer-to-peer and wire transfers (MCC 4829).
7. **E-Wallet Personal Transfers**: Personal transfers via e-wallets like Alipay, WeChat, etc. (MCC 7299).
8. **Retroactive Card Reassignment**: Third-party card reassignment or retroactive flex transfers (e.g., Curve ReFi/Flex - MCC 6555).
9. **Canceled / Refunded Purchases**: Any transaction that is voided, canceled, or refunded.

### Refund & Points Reversal Policy
* When a purchase is canceled or refunded, the system automatically reverses the associated Rewards Points.
* **If points were already spent/redeemed**: The system calculates `Deducted Amount = Number of Points Used × Current Point Redemption Value` and automatically deducts the equivalent value in tokens or fiat from the user's account.
* **Cap Policy**: Monthly point caps are **NOT restored** upon a refund.

### Mechanism Transition Rules
* **Old Cashback Mechanism**: Applies to cards applied for before June 3, 2026 UTC (migrated Aug 4, 2026).
* **New Cashback Mechanism**: Effective for all card applications submitted from **June 15, 2026 UTC onwards**.

### Common Customer Issues
* **Points Missing Post-Purchase**: Explain that points remain in *Pending* status for 4 calendar days before becoming available.
* **Negative Point Balance or Unexpected Token Deduction**: Caused by points reversal following a merchant refund where points were already redeemed.
* **Zero Points Earned on Specific Transaction**: Transaction fell under an excluded Merchant Category Code (MCC) such as crypto purchases, ATM cash, e-wallet top-ups, or Curve transfers.
* **Rewards Market Redemption Failure**: Common causes include insufficient available points or items marked "Out of Stock" (redemption limit reached).

## 5. How to Activate Auto Cashback from Bybit Card

### Mechanics & Eligibility
Auto Cashback automatically converts accumulated Rewards Points into a designated cryptocurrency or fiat token and credits it directly to the user's Funding Account.

* **Eligibility**: Available to all Bybit Card holders (Virtual Card Lite, Virtual Card, and Physical Card).
* **Minimum Conversion Threshold**: **50 Rewards Points** minimum required to trigger execution.
* **Conversion Rate**: Points × 0.002 EUR, converted to chosen token at Bybit EU's prevailing reference exchange rate.
* **Payout Schedule**: Processed daily at approximately **2:00 AM UTC**.
* **Payout Destination**: **Funding Account**.
* **Default Fallback**: If the selected token is removed or unavailable during payout execution, cashback defaults to **USDC**.

### Step-by-Step Activation Guide

#### On the Mobile App / Web
1. Log in to your Bybit EU Account.
2. Go to **Finance** → **Card**.
3. Click **View Rewards** / **Rewards Market**.
4. Locate the **Auto Cashback** toggle and switch it **ON**.
5. Select your preferred payout asset (e.g., BTC, ETH, USDC, EUR).
6. Confirm choices.

### Common Customer Issues
* **Auto Cashback Did Not Execute at 2:00 AM**: Customer accumulated fewer than 50 available points. Execution will occur once 50 points threshold is reached.
* **Received USDC Instead of Chosen Token (e.g., BTC)**: Selected token was temporarily unavailable or undergoing maintenance during the 2:00 AM batch run; system defaulted to USDC.
* **Cannot Find Cashback in Account**: Cashback is deposited into the **Funding Account**, not Trading or Spot accounts.
* **Cashback Stopped**: Customer reached their VIP tier's monthly maximum cashback cap.

## 6. How Bybit Card Transactions Work

### Fund Deduction & Conversion Hierarchy
When a transaction is initiated, Bybit EU evaluates the Funding Account balances in a multi-tier hierarchy:

1. **Primary Deductible Balance**: Available **Fiat Currency (EUR)** in Funding Account.
2. **Secondary Deductible Balance**: User's selected **Payment Crypto Asset** (e.g., USDC, USDT, BTC, ETH, XRP).
3. **Execution Logic**:
   * **Sufficient Fiat**: Full amount deducted from Fiat EUR.
   * **Insufficient Fiat, Sufficient Crypto**: All available Fiat EUR is consumed first. Remaining balance is paid by converting selected Crypto.
   * **Minimum Crypto Conversion**: **1 EUR** minimum equivalent. If remaining crypto to convert is less than 1 EUR, transaction is declined.
   * **Insufficient Total Funds**: Transaction is **DECLINED**.

### Authorization, Holds & Settlement
* **Pre-authorization Freeze**: Upon authorization, transaction funds are frozen in the Funding Account.
* **Merchant Settlement Window**: Merchants have up to **30 days** to complete settlement. Funds are formally deducted only when settlement finishes.
* **Authorization Reversal**: If a merchant cancels or fails to settle within 30 days, frozen funds are automatically released back to available balance.

### Exchange Rates, Fees & FX Padding
* **Foreign Exchange (FX) Fee**: **0.5%** charged on top of Mastercard's reference exchange rate for non-EUR transactions.
* **Crypto Conversion Fee**: **0.9%** charged on top of Bybit EU's One-Click Sell exchange rate.
* **FX Padding**: A temporary security buffer added to the frozen authorized amount to protect against exchange rate volatility prior to merchant settlement. Any unused padding is fully refunded to available balance upon settlement.

### Common Customer Issues
* **Frozen Balance Higher Than Receipt Amount**: Caused by temporary FX padding applied during pre-authorization. Difference is released upon final merchant settlement.
* **Transaction Declined Despite Sufficient Crypto Total**: Crypto balance after partial fiat deduction was worth less than the **1 EUR minimum conversion threshold**.
* **Pending Authorization Stuck**: Pre-authorization holds last up to 30 days unless canceled earlier by the merchant.

## 7. Bybit Card Transactions FAQ

### Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) & PSD2 Compliance
Under European Union PSD2 regulations, Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) enforces security controls on contactless and online transactions.

* **Contactless SCA Triggers (Requires Inserting Card & PIN Entry)**:
  1. Single contactless payment > **50 EUR**.
  2. Cumulative contactless total > **150 EUR** since last SCA PIN entry.
  3. **More than 5 consecutive contactless transactions** without PIN verification.
* **Counter Reset**: Successful Chip & PIN entry at a retail terminal or PIN entry at an ATM resets all contactless SCA counters to zero.

### 3D Secure (3DS) Online Authentication
* **Primary 3DS Method**: Biometric authentication (Face ID or Fingerprint) via Bybit EU App push notification within **120 seconds**.
* **Biometrics Not Enabled**: Prompted to enable biometrics within 60 seconds (up to 4 attempts).
* **Fallback Authentication**: 5th attempt redirects to **Email OTP Code + Security Question verification**.
* **Manual Switch**: Users can click **Receive Email** on the 3DS screen to switch directly to Email OTP + Security Question verification.

### Declined Transaction Troubleshooting Matrix

| Declined Reason | Root Cause | Required Action / Support Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Insufficient Spending Power** | Funds located in UTA, Earn, or Derivatives account instead of Funding Account. | Transfer required fiat/crypto to **Funding Account**. |
| **Card is Locked / Suspended** | Card or account suspended by risk/compliance. | Submit support inquiry via webform under *General Usage Inquiry → My Bybit Card is suspended*. |
| **This Card Has Expired** | Physical or Virtual card past expiration date. | Renew card on Card Management page. |
| **Exceeded Daily/Monthly Spend Limit** | Transaction exceeds tier spending limits. | Check dashboard limits or submit a Tier Upgrade request. |
| **This Card is Not Activated** | Physical card CVV not entered on dashboard. | Go to Card Dashboard → Manage My Card → Activate. |
| **Transaction Type Not Allowed** | Merchant in restricted category (gambling, security brokers, crypto exchanges). | Merchant category unsupported by policy; user must use alternative payment method. |
| **Incorrect CVV / Expiry / PIN** | User typed wrong security details. | Re-enter details. If locked after 3 attempts, unblock CVV or Reset PIN on Card Management page. |
| **SCA / Terminal PIN Required** | Terminal rejected contactless due to PSD2 SCA limit. | Instruct user to insert physical card into terminal and enter 4-digit PIN. |

### Disputes & Unauthorized Transactions
* **Unauthorized Charges**: Freeze card immediately, report card lost/stolen, and submit a Transaction Dispute form via Support Hub within **60 days** of transaction date.
* **Installments**: Bybit Card does **NOT** support installment payments.

### Common Customer Issues
* **3DS Notification Timeout**: Occurs when mobile app notifications are disabled in phone OS settings.
* **Contactless Suddenly Rejected at Store**: Reached 5-transaction or 150 EUR cumulative SCA limit. Customer must insert card and enter PIN.
* **Email 3DS Security Question Failure**: Wrong security answer provided. User must reset security questions in Card Settings.

## 9. How to Make Payments with Bybit Card

### Payment Methods & Guidelines

#### A. Virtual Card / Virtual Card Lite Online Payments
1. Retrieve card details (16-digit card number, CVV, expiry date) from **Card Dashboard** → **View Number**.
2. Input card details on online merchant checkout page.
3. Authenticate transaction via 3DS prompt in Bybit EU App (biometric verification or email OTP + security question within 120s).
4. Return to merchant checkout to complete payment.

#### B. Physical Card In-Store & Online Payments
* **In-Store Contactless**: Tap card on NFC terminal (amounts <= 50 EUR, subject to SCA limits).
* **In-Store Chip & PIN**: Insert physical card into terminal reader and enter 4-digit PIN (required for first purchase, large purchases, or SCA resets).
* **Online Payments**: Enter card details printed on card or app dashboard + 3DS authorization.

#### C. Mobile Wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay)
* Adding Bybit Card to Apple Wallet, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay is fully supported.
* **Setup Path**: Open mobile wallet app → Add Debit Card → Enter Bybit Card details → Verify via SMS/email OTP.
* Digital wallet payments bypass physical card chip requirements and use device biometrics for SCA.

### Managing Security Toggles in Settings
Users can configure operational toggles under **Card Dashboard** → **More Settings**:

| Toggle Option | Description | Default Setting (Virtual) | Default Setting (Physical) | Security Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Online Transactions** | Internet-based transactions. | **ON** | **ON** | Enabling requires 2FA; disabling does not. |
| **Non-3DS Online Transactions** | Online purchases without 3DS prompt. | **ON** | **OFF** | Enabling requires 2FA; disabling does not. |
| **ATM Withdrawals** | Cash withdrawals at ATMs. | **OFF** | **ON** | Enabling requires 2FA; disabling does not. |

### Common Customer Issues
* **Physical Card Online Purchase Declined**: Merchant does not support 3DS and customer's physical card has **Non-3DS Online Transactions** toggled OFF by default. User must enable toggle in More Settings.
* **Mobile Wallet Setup Failure**: Ensure registered mobile phone number matches card profile settings.

## 10. How to Link or Remove Your Bank Card

### Overview & Restrictions
*Note: This feature refers to linking an external personal Visa/Mastercard bank card to a Bybit EU account to buy crypto via One-Click Buy / Fiat Deposit (not to be confused with the Bybit Card itself).*

* **Jurisdictional Restriction**: Linking external bank cards is **NOT available to Austrian nationals, residents, or tax residents**.
* **Linking Cap**: Maximum **5 bank cards** can be linked per Bybit EU account.
* **Name Matching Mandatory**: The external bank cardholder name **MUST strictly match** the verified KYC name on Bybit EU.
* **Address Matching**: Billing address entered must match the address registered with the card-issuing bank.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### A. Link a New Bank Card
1. Go to **Buy Crypto** → **One-Click Buy**.
2. Input order amount and click **Next**.
3. Select **Bank Card** as payment method.
4. Click **Buy With EUR**.
5. Input card details: Email, First & Last Name, Card Number, Expiration Date, CVV.
6. Input Billing Address matching bank card records.
7. Confirm purchase order. *Card details are saved only after order confirmation.*

#### B. Remove a Linked Bank Card
1. Go to **Buy Crypto** → **One-Click Buy**.
2. Click the three-dot menu (upper right) → **Payment Method** → **One-Click Buy**.
3. Select card to remove → Click **Remove card**.

### Common Customer Issues
* **Card Linking Failed due to Name Mismatch**: Customer attempting to use a card belonging to a spouse/friend. Third-party card linking is strictly prohibited.
* **Issuer Declined**: Card issuing bank blocked international/crypto purchases. Customer must contact bank.
* **Reached 5-Card Cap**: Customer must remove an existing card before linking a new one.

## 11. Card Security & Management

### Comprehensive Security Controls

#### A. Freeze / Unfreeze Card
* **Freeze**: Go to **Card Management** → click **Freeze** → **Confirm**. Instantly blocks all authorization requests and suspends linked digital wallets.
* **Unfreeze**: Go to **Card Management** → click **Unfreeze** → complete 2FA security verification.

#### B. Resetting Card PIN
1. Go to **Card Management** → click **Reset PIN**.
2. Complete 2FA security verification.
3. Enter new 4-digit PIN and confirm.
4. **MANDATORY POST-RESET INITIALIZATION**:
   * Complete an ATM transaction first (e.g., **Balance Inquiry**) with the new PIN.
   * Complete a physical retail purchase using chip & PIN with the new PIN.
   * Contactless will function only after completing these steps.
*(Note: Virtual and Physical cards maintain independent PINs).*

#### C. Unblocking Locked CVV or PIN
* If CVV or PIN is entered incorrectly **3 times**, the card locks for security.
* **Resolution**: Go to **Card Management** → click **CVV Unblock** or **Reset PIN** → complete 2FA verification.

#### D. Replacing Physical Card
Go to **Card Management** → **Replace Card** → select reason:
1. **Lost or Compromised (fraud, stolen, etc.)**: Old physical card is permanently terminated immediately. 5 EUR replacement fee applies. Linked Apple/Google Pay cards are suspended.
2. **Damaged or Doesn't Work**: New card dispatched. Old card remains active until new card arrives and is activated.

#### E. Terminating Card
* Go to **Card Management** → click **Terminate** → complete 2FA verification → confirm.
* Permanent cancellation. Unused Rewards Points remain accessible on Rewards Market.

#### F. Emergency Lost/Stolen Procedure
1. Customer must **Freeze Card immediately** via app/web.
2. Go to **Card Management** → **Replace Card** → select **Lost or Compromised**.
3. Review Transaction History for fraudulent charges.
4. Submit Transaction Dispute via Support Hub within **60 days**.
5. If customer cannot log in, direct to **Live Chat Support** immediately for emergency account lock.

### Common Customer Issues
* **New PIN Fails at Retail Stores**: Customer forgot to run an ATM balance inquiry to complete post-reset initialization.
* **CVV Locked Error**: Customer attempted 3 incorrect online CVV entries; instruct to use **CVV Unblock** on Card Dashboard.
